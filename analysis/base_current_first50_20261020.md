# Real base current and first-50-frame fit (2026-10-20)

Recording: Glorbo4 `door_policy_replay_20261019_080430.pt`, frames 0–49. The recorded base action is raw; the deployed command multiplies it by 0.1. The recording's motor diagnostics report 25 A drive and 40 A steer limits. The checked-out controller source says 20 A drive, so the recorded limit is used for this run and the discrepancy must be resolved before treating either as a hardware specification.

With Kraken X60 FOC `Kt=0.01981 Nm/A`, drive ratio `(50/14)*(19/25)*(45/15)=8.142857`, and wheel radius 0.0508 m: 25 A means 0.4953 Nm at one drive rotor, 4.033 Nm at its wheel, and 79.38 N of ideal wheel force. Four aligned wheels have an ideal 317.5 N translation bound. Four tangential forces at caster offset radius 0.2552 m have an ideal 81.0 Nm drive-only yaw bound. At the controller source's 20 A limit, those become 254.0 N and 64.8 Nm. Gear and tire losses lower the usable values. The 40 A steer limit gives 0.7924 Nm per steer rotor or 10.14 Nm after its 12.8 reduction; that is caster steering-joint torque, not chassis yaw torque. Exact chassis wrench also depends on caster angles and the drive/steer coupling. The recording does not log caster angles.

The first 50 frames contain 35 sufficiently fresh, unique motor diagnostic samples. Converting current to ideal wheel force and projecting it using inferred wheel travel direction gives a longitudinal speed-error slope of 396 Ns/m over the whole window, 298 Ns/m over frames 15–49, and 217 Ns/m over frames 27–49. The full-window yaw slope is 21 Nms/rad with low explanatory power (R² 0.18); the y slope is negative, so y is not identified physically. These are empirical equivalent slopes, not measured hardware PD gains. Ruckig output, caster angles, motor friction, gearing losses, and inertia are unobserved.

The branches therefore sample uncertain equivalent gains. Both retain conservative sim effort caps of 110–160 N for translation and 35–55 Nm for yaw, below the ideal current-derived bounds. This does not assert those caps were measured directly.
